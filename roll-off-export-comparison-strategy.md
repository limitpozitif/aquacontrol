## 3. US STRATEGY — COMPETITIVENESS WITHOUT COMPROMISING QUALITY

### 3.1 Product standardization (market fit = quality, not waste)

| Action | Effect | Quality impact |
|---|---|---|
| Width 2,500 → 2,440 mm (8 ft) | Fits a standard 40 ft container/flat rack → freight/unit drops ~15–20% | None (8 ft is already the US standard) |
| US hooklift compliance (Galbreath/Perkins hook, 18–20 ft skid) | Direct fleet compatibility → lower rejection/adaptation risk | Increases (full spec compliance) |
| Position as 30 m³ → 40 yd³ (~30.6 m³) | US buyers classify the product correctly | None |
| **Nestable (nested stacking) body design** | 2–3 levels on vessel → freight/unit drops ~40–50% | Increases (FEM reinforcement; sloped wall eases emptying) |

### 3.2 Logistics and freight optimization

- Regular service agreement with the **Port of Savannah/Brunswick**; for high volumes, replace RoRo/breakbulk with **container + nestable packing** → freight per unit can drop from $2,200 to ~$1,400.
- **Offer FOB instead of DDP:** freight and FX risk shared with the distributor.
- **Shipment consolidation:** full loads with 4–8 week batched orders; group shipments by customer.

### 3.3 Section 232 tariff strategy (the clause that decides the project)

| Option | How it works | Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| **Correct HTS classification** | Product-specific customs counsel opinion (7326.90 vs 7309 vs 8716); some manufactured steel articles are out of scope | ~3% duty instead of 25% |
| **Product-specific exclusion (PSE)** | Exclusion petition to the US Department of Commerce | Legal 25% exemption |
| **KD (knock-down) assembly in the US** | Steel parts made in TR, welding/assembly on a small US line (~$0.5–0.8M) → "Made in USA" | Tariff limits + approach to Buy America criteria |
| **Mexico assembly (USMCA)** | TR parts → MX assembly → zero-duty US entry (if USMCA rules met) | 0% duty |

> **Recommendation:** obtain an HTS opinion and file the PSE before the first pilot shipment; if the 25% tariff stays, switch to the KD assembly scenario.

### 3.4 Quality perception and certification (breaking the "cheap Turkish goods" perception)

- **AWS D1.1:** All welders and procedures certified to the American welding standard → a direct answer to US customer specifications.
- **ISO 9001 + AISC/manufacturer quality program:** factory audit reports added to tender documents.
- **Warranty line in the US:** on-site warranty through a distributor/service network — a "quality + service" package (reduces quality recall risk).
- **Test/report package:** load test (FEM + physical), weld inspection, coating thickness — delivered with every shipment.

### 3.5 Market entry (low sunk-risk)

- Production in TR + **small warehouse/sales office in Georgia** (not a plant) → stocked fast delivery and on-site contact.
- **Channels:** hooklift dealers, solid waste companies (WM, Republic, Waste Pro regional), rental companies; a "Made in USA" KD variant for public tenders.

## 4. EU STRATEGY — COMPETITIVENESS WITHOUT COMPROMISING QUALITY

### 4.1 Certification (quality assurance = entry condition)

- **EN 1090-1 (EXC2/EXC3):** CE marking for structural steel products — though not always mandatory, it is a prerequisite for German/Austrian buyers. Strong quality signal.
- **TÜV/GS inspection** and **EN ISO 9606**-certified welders.
- **ISO 9001** + technical file with every product (welding plan, material certificates, test results).

### 4.2 CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism) management — full application from 2026

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Low-carbon steel sourcing** (EAF/scrap-based, low embedded CO₂) | CBAM certificate cost drops from ~$650 to ~$300 (CBAM is EUR-denominated; values shown in USD equivalent) |
| **CBAM reporting infrastructure** (embedded emissions documentation) | Correct tax base; zero penalty risk |
| **Track Turkey's ETS** (entry into force 2027+) | Burden decreases gradually as the domestic carbon price is deducted from CBAM |
| **Long-life coating** (HDG/2K paint option) | Quality rises, warranty/customer satisfaction → pushes the price ceiling up |

### 4.3 Product adaptation (Europe = quality standard, not waste)

- **German/Austrian hook systems** (Abrollkipper) and Swiss/French variants — 2.5 m width is already standard in Europe (truck 2.55 m) → **no logistics or fit issue**.
- **Special variants:** drain-valve, fire/concrete demolition container, compactor-compatible, stainless-lined → €8,000–11,000 premium band.
- **Short lead time (6–8 weeks):** China 12+ weeks, near-Polish levels — lead time justifies the premium price.

### 4.4 Positioning and channels

- **Do not enter the standard market** (€7,000 band; Polish competition). **Compete at the premium/custom end** (€8,000–11,000).
- **Channels:** Abfallwirtschaft companies (Germany/Austria), municipal contracts, rental fleets, OEM agreements with hooklift manufacturers.
- **Logistics:** 2 units per truck, Izmir/Trieste RoRo, group shipments — freight ~$1,200/unit.

## 5. COMMON PRINCIPLE — COST REDUCTION WITHOUT COMPROMISING QUALITY

These principles apply to both US and EU exports; they reduce cost **without reducing material thickness** (6 mm floor and 4 mm wall are preserved).

| Principle | How | Gain |
|---|---|---|
| **FEM design optimization** | Reduce weight 5–10% (same structural strength; added reinforcement points) | Lower material + freight, higher quality |
| **Nestable/stackable body** | 2–3 levels on vessel | Freight ~40–50% lower |
| **Phase 3 automation** (robots + CNC plasma) | Consistent welding, low rework | Unit cost ~6% lower, quality standardized |
| **Few variants, high series** | Jig/robot efficiency; single loading configuration | Efficiency +15–20% |
| **Bulk steel + scrap hedge** | Long-term supply contract | Lower price risk |
| **Long-life coating** (HDG/2K) | Lower warranty and service cost | Lower total cost of ownership (premium defense) |
| **Certification set** (AWS D1.1 / EN 1090 / ISO 9001) | Opens tender and distributor doors | Margin protected, no need to cut price |

| Step | Timing | Action |

| 1 | Months 0–3 | Phase 1 production at the TR plant + **obtain HTS classification and Section 232 opinion** (counsel) |

| 5 | Months 18–36 | Trigger Phase 3 robotic investment with export volume; run the EU premium line and US channels in parallel |

**Decision logic:** Export decisions rest on **real pilot shipment data**; no large sunk investment is made in any market in advance. A US plant ($3.37M) only comes up if Buy America / local-preference channels are won.
