## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This project establishes a manufacturing facility for **30 m³ heavy-duty Roll-Off containers** (6,000 × 2,500 × 2,200 mm, open top, hook bar, skid rails). Production is set up in a **three-phase phased model** rather than a single large build: each phase is financed from its own cash flow, and a go/no-go decision gate exists at every stage.

| Metric | Value (base case) |
|---|---|
| Total investment (3 phases) | **₺84.5 million (~$1.78M)** |
| Phase 1 investment | **₺30 million (~$632K)** |
| Year 1 unit sales | 268 units |
| Year 3 unit sales | 650 units |
| Unit production cost (Phase 1) | ~₺317,000 (~$6,674) |
| Average selling price | ₺500–545 thousand (~$10,526–11,474) (entry → normalization) |
| Annual EBITDA (Y1 / Y2 / Y3) | **₺50M / ₺107M / ₺161M (~$1.05M / ~$2.25M / ~$3.39M)** |
| EBITDA margin | ~38% (entry) → 46% (Y3) |
| Simple payback | **~15 months** (from production start) |
| Cumulative free cash flow (3 years) | **~₺159M (~$3.35M) (~1.9× investment)** |

**Market entry strategy (price):** Enter at **₺500,000 (~$10,526)** — ~13% below domestic competitors; growth comes from volume. Price normalizes to ₺520,000 (~$10,947) in Y2 and ₺545,000 (~$11,474) in Y3. With ~₺311,000 (~$6,547) unit cash cost, entry margin is ~38%; scale + automation push it to ~46% by Y3.

**Why this product?** Roll-off containers serve the excavation, construction, municipal and industrial waste markets with steady demand. They are low-technology but high-value-added products. Local production competes with imports on short lead time, customization, warranty and "domestic product" status in public tenders. Customs protection on steel products limits the import price advantage.

## 4. PRODUCTION STRATEGY — THREE-PHASE ROADMAP

The model is not a single large build; it is **three independent phases**. Each phase finances the next from the cash flow it generates, and every phase ends with a clear **go/no-go gate**: if the condition is not met, no further investment is made and the existing operation is continued at reduced scale. The phases are aligned to years, so the investor can see at a glance what is spent in each phase and what revenue has been generated up to that point.

### Phase comparison

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timing | Year 1 (months 1–12) | Year 2 (months 13–24) | Year 3 (months 25–36) |
| Capacity | 15–20 → 40 units/month | 45 units/month | 55–60 units/month |
| Staff | 15 (9 direct / 6 indirect) | 20 (12 / 8) | 19 (10 / 9) |
| Unit cost | ~₺317,000 (~$6,674) | ~₺314,000 (~$6,611) | ~₺306,000 (~$6,442) |
| Key change | Manual welding + external cutting | CNC plasma brought in-house | Robot welding goes live |

### Phase 1 — Year 1 (months 1–12): start production with low capital

- **Investment:** ₺30M (~$632K) — workshop fit-out, overhead cranes (2 × 5 t), 2 welding stations (MIG/MAG), paint booth, portable cutting kit (~₺250k ≈ $5,263), fixtures/jigs, initial stock.
- **Method / what changes:** Cutting and bending are done at an **external supplier** (no capital required); manual welding and assembly in-house. Production starts at 15–20 units/month and ramps to ~36–40 by year-end. Building a customer portfolio realistically takes ~1 year, so no headcount is kept for 40 units/month at the start (4 welders, 1 salesperson).
- **Objective:** Validate demand with real sales, learn operations, build customer/hook-type database.

**Financial position so far (end of Year 1):**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue | ₺134M (~$2.82M) (268 units × ₺500,000 ≈ $10,526) |
| EBITDA | ₺50.7M (~$1.07M) (38%) |
| Cumulative investment (capex + working capital) | ₺33.5M (~$705K) |
| **Cumulative free cash flow** | **+₺5.2M (~$109K)** |

**Gate 1 (month 12):** If sustained sales are **≥ 30 units/month** by year-end (ramp ends ~36) → proceed to Phase 2. Otherwise: reduce capacity, make no further investment.

### Phase 2 — Year 2 (months 13–24): bring CNC plasma cutting in-house

- **Investment:** +₺6M (~$126K) — CNC plasma + gas infrastructure (payback ~7.5 months).
- **What changes:** External cutting dependency **ends**; lead time shortens, unit cost drops from ~₺317,000 (~$6,674) to ~₺314,000 (~$6,611). Capacity rises to 45 units/month; welders 4 → 6, staff 20.

**Financial position so far (end of Year 2):**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Cumulative revenue | ₺394M (~$8.29M) (134 + 260) |
| Cumulative EBITDA | ₺157.2M (~$3.31M) |
| Cumulative investment | ₺73.5M (~$1.55M) |
| **Cumulative free cash flow** | **+₺46.7M (~$983K)** |

**Gate 2 (month 24):** If sustained sales are **≥ 45 units/month** → proceed to Phase 3.

### Phase 3 — Year 3 (months 25–36): welding robots and automation

- **Investment:** +₺48.5M (~$1.02M) → **total ₺84.5M (~$1.78M)** (₺30M + ₺6M + ₺48.5M)
  - 6 × 6-DOF welding robots: ₺18M (~$379K); EOAT/profile gripper + automatic torch changer (in-house design, material): ₺1M (~$21K) → subtotal ₺19M (~$400K)
  - Positioner/rollover fixtures (chassis turner, body rollover, door positioner, synchronization): ₺9.5M (~$200K)
  - Jigs/locating fixtures: ₺4M (~$84K)
  - Commissioning, training, contingency: ₺16M (~$337K)
- **What changes:** Robots take over most welding work → welders 6 → 2; a single robot operator + a welding engineer are added (staff 20 → 19). Unit cost falls to ~₺306,000 (~$6,442), capacity rises to 55–60 units/month; consistent weld quality and resilience to welder shortage.

**Financial position so far (end of Year 3):**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|

| Cumulative EBITDA | ₺318.4M (~$6.70M) |
| Cumulative investment | ₺84.5M (~$1.78M) |
| **Cumulative free cash flow** | **+₺158.9M (~$3.35M) (~1.9× investment)** |

> **Why phased?** Robots create a depreciation burden at low volume (a one-shot fully automated plant can be more expensive than manual at low sales). Each phase is financed from its own cash flow; there is no single-shot stranded-asset risk for the investor.

## 6. PRICING AND REVENUE

### Competitive entry price (penetration)
- **Entry price:** **₺500,000/unit (~$10,526)** — ~13% below domestic competitors (~₺575,000 ≈ $12,105). Sits above the duty-included import; justified to customers by short lead time + warranty + customization.
- **Growth engine is volume, not price:** the competitive price starts market share and reference wins; scale lowers unit cost.
- **Normalization path:** Y2 ₺520,000 (~$10,947) → Y3 ₺545,000 (~$11,474) (gradual, as brand and references take hold).
- Options: rear doors, custom dimensions, corporate livery, special paint → +₺15–40 thousand/unit (~$316–842)

**Sales channels:** Municipalities and public tenders ("domestic product" advantage), excavation/environmental contractors, construction companies, and co-selling with hook-lift truck dealers.

## 8. SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS

| Scenario | EBITDA margin | Payback estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Base | ~38% (entry) → 46% (Y3) | ~15 months |
| Price −15% | ~27% | ~23 months |
| Volume −25% | ~33% | ~19 months |
| Price −15% and volume −25% | ~23% | ~29 months |

The model is most sensitive to **price** (steel is not the top lever because the steel share of unit cost is low relative to the price share). If steel rises +20%, unit cost rises from ~₺317,000 (~$6,674) to ~₺343,000 (~$7,221); the margin can be protected via price adjustment.

## 12. INVESTOR VALUE PROPOSITION

1. **Low-risk start:** Production begins with the first ₺30M (~$632K); the full ₺84.5M (~$1.78M) is not committed before demand is proven.
2. **Self-financed scaling:** Later phases are financed from the current phase's cash flow; external debt needs are low.
3. **Protected market:** Local production operates under customs protection vs imports; "domestic product" status in public tenders.
4. **Geographic advantage:** 2–5 days freight to Europe / Middle East / North Africa exports (30+ days from China).
5. **Competitive entry, short payback:** ~38% entry margin (Y1) → ~46% normalization margin (Y3); ~15-month simple payback.
6. **Clear exit options:** Stop/downsize option at every phase; limited stranded-asset risk.
7. **Price penetration:** Enter ~13% below domestic competitors; growth comes from volume, price normalizes in Y2/Y3.
